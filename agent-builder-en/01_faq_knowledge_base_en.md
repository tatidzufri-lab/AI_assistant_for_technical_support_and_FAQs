# Knowledge base: Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

## 1. What the retreat is
**What is the Know Yourself retreat?**
An intimate 5-day program for women in the mountains of the Dolomites, South Tyrol, Italy. It is for those who want to slow down, hear themselves and regain inner clarity.

**How long is the trip, and is there any preparation?**
The in-person trip to the mountains lasts 5 days. Participation also includes online preparation before the start: video modules, exercises and a support chat.

**Is there any esotericism, tarot or astrology in the program?**
No. We rely only on scientific existential analysis, art therapy and mindfulness meditation. No magic and no esotericism.

**Who is the retreat for?**
- Women who are tired of multitasking and feel burned out.
- Mothers whose children have grown up and who are looking for new meaning beyond family roles.
- Managers and business owners used to controlling everything.
- Women at a crossroads in life or in a creative crisis.

## 2. Terms, accommodation and travel
**Where is the retreat, and how do I get there?**
The program takes place in the mountains of the Dolomites (South Tyrol, Italy). Participants buy their own tickets to Innsbruck Airport (INN). On the first day of the program, an organised transfer picks the group up at the airport arrivals hall at 12:00. The drive to the venue takes 1.5–2 hours.

**How many people are in the group? Can a man join?**
The program is for women only. The group is strictly limited to 10 participants to keep it intimate and trusting.

**What is included in the price?**
The full format includes accommodation, meals, the transfer from the airport, all practices, art therapy, workbooks and materials.

**What should I bring?**
- Clothes: sweatpants, leggings, loose T-shirts. No evening dresses needed.
- Warm layers: a fleece, a windbreaker, warm socks (it can get cold in the mountains).
- Shoes: hiking boots or trainers with a good grip, plus comfortable indoor shoes.
- Other: sunscreen (SPF 30+) and a personal first-aid kit. We provide notebooks and pens.

## 3. Program and rules
**What will we do? Are there hard hikes or excursions?**
There are no excursions and no exhausting hikes. The schedule has morning practices, art therapy, meditation, group sessions and light restorative walks in nature.

**Do I have to take part in everything?**
The main rule is to be gentle with yourself. If a participant wants to skip a practice and just rest, she is completely free to do so. The schedule also has time for silence and being alone.

**Is there internet?**
There may be a connection, but we strongly recommend a "digital detox". We ask participants to warn their colleagues in advance and not to work in the evenings. You can contact your family, but it is better to set aside a short time for it. Filming other participants is strictly forbidden.
