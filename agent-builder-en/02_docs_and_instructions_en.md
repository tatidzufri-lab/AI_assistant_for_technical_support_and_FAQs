# Documentation and internal rules

## 1. Booking and payment
- **Booking:** A place is reserved only after a 30% deposit of the total price is paid.
- **Balance:** The remaining 70% must be paid no later than 14 days before the in-person trip.
- **Refund policy:**
  - Cancellation more than 30 days before the start: 100% of the deposit is refunded.
  - Cancellation 14 to 30 days before the start: the deposit is kept but can be moved to the next retreat (once).
  - Cancellation less than 14 days before the start: no refund, because accommodation and transfer are already paid for.

## 2. Check-in
- **Time:** Check-in at the mountain venue is on the day of arrival, from 14:00 to 16:00.
- **Rooms:** Participants stay in comfortable twin rooms, two people per room (unless they booked the format with a single room).
- **Meals:** Three healthy meals a day. Participants must tell us about allergies and food intolerances (vegetarian, gluten-free, lactose-free) 2 weeks before the trip.

## 3. Privacy policy and guardrails
- Personal stories shared in group sessions must never be passed on to third parties ("what happens here stays here").
- If clients or competitors ask about internal metrics (landing page conversion, cost per lead, the sales funnel, the marketing view of the target audience or the tone of voice), support politely declines and explains that internal business processes are confidential.
