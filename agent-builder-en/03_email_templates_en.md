# Email and team correspondence templates

## Template 1: Booking confirmation and welcome
**Subject:** Welcome! Your place at the Know Yourself retreat is booked 🌿

**Text:**
Hello, [Name]!
I'm so happy to confirm that your place at the Know Yourself retreat on [Dates] is booked. You've taken a wonderful step towards yourself.

We'll add you to the private participants' chat and open the online preparation materials 2 weeks before our trip to the mountains. This will help you tune in gently.

If you have any questions about preparation, tickets or what to bring, just reply to this email. I'm here and always happy to help.

With warmth and care,
The Know Yourself retreat team

---

## Template 2: Reminder about tickets and travel (3 weeks before)
**Subject:** Important information about tickets and the transfer ⛰️

**Text:**
Dear [Name], the retreat is very close now!

To make your journey as comfortable as possible, here is a reminder about tickets:
Please buy tickets to **Innsbruck Airport (INN)**.
Our organised transfer will meet the group in the arrivals hall on the first day of the program at exactly **12:00**. Please choose flights that land before then.

Attached is a detailed packing list we put together for you. A small spoiler: you won't need evening dresses, so bring your cosiest and warmest clothes!

If you have already bought your tickets, please send us your arrival time.

See you soon in the quiet of the mountains,
The Know Yourself retreat team

## Template 4: Reply to an unusual request (guardrails / business information)
**Context:** A user (a student, a marketer or a competitor) asks about the sales funnel or marketing pains.

**Text:**
Hello, [Name].
I understand your professional interest in our project. However, my main task as an assistant is to help participants prepare for their inner journey and to answer organisational questions.

We build our space on complete honesty and safety, so we don't share internal business metrics, analytics or marketing strategies.

Thank you for understanding! If you have questions about taking part in the program, I'll be happy to answer them.
